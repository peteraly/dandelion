# Offerings by organisation

sources only. "check" means a detail could not be confirmed; confirm it with the organisation before
telling anyone it is available. Listing an organisation here does not mean it works with Dandelion Foundation.

## Afya Call Centre 199 (Ministry of Health)

- **Afya Call Centre 199** (helpline). Free health information, advice and referral to a health facility by voice call or SMS (Kiswahili and English).
  - Who: Anyone in Tanzania. How: Call or SMS 199 from any network; Zanzibar has its own line, 115. Cost: Free.
  - Where: Mainland national (Tanga included); Zanzibar uses 115. Status: Running (confirmed 2025). Sources: [1](https://findahelpline.com/organizations/199-afya-call-center) · [2](https://www.villagereach.org/2025/08/18/tanzanias-afya-call-center-cita/)

## National Child Helpline 116 (C-Sema)

- **National Child Helpline 116** (helpline). Free, confidential counselling for children and young people; referral to social welfare officers and Police Gender and Children's Desks; covers abuse, GBV, mental health, sexual and reproductive health.
  - Who: Children and young people, and adults worried about a child. How: Call 116 free from any network. Cost: Free.
  - Where: National; mainland 24 hours, Zanzibar 12 hours Monday to Friday. Status: Running (2.33 million calls handled in 2024). Sources: [1](https://sematanzania.org/child-helpline) · [2](https://lifeline-international.com/member/tanzania/)

## Police Gender and Children's Desks

- **Gender and Children's Desks** (helpline). Confidential place to report violence or abuse at a police station; help with legal steps and referral to health and social welfare.
  - Who: Women, girls and children who face violence, and their families. How: Go to a police station with a Gender and Children's Desk; or call 116 for referral. Cost: Free.
  - Where: National; new desks opened in 2025 (for example Madema, Zanzibar and Bugarama, Shinyanga). Status: Running (2025). Sources: [1](https://tanzania.unfpa.org/en/news/transforming-survivor-support-%E2%80%94-silence-and-stigma-protection-and-dignity-tanzania)
- **One Stop Centres (survivor care)** (clinic or health service). Medical care, legal help and social welfare support in one place for survivors of gender-based violence.
  - Who: Survivors of violence, any age. How: At hospitals with a One Stop Centre; via police desks or 116. Cost: Free.
  - Where: Selected hospitals (list check). Status: Running (2025). Sources: [1](https://tanzania.unfpa.org/en/news/transforming-survivor-support-%E2%80%94-silence-and-stigma-protection-and-dignity-tanzania)

  - Who: Adolescent girls and young women. How: Through BRAC Tanzania branches and community groups. Cost: Free.
  - Where: Multiple regions (check Tanga). Status: Running (337,525 participants reported; date check). Sources: [1](https://bracusa.org/?p=7452) · [2](https://bracinternational.org/en/where-we-work/tanzania/)
- **BRAC Tanzania Finance microloans** (loan or guarantee). Small loans for women's businesses, mainly through groups.
  - Who: Women in business (check criteria). How: BRAC Tanzania Finance branches. Cost: Interest (rate not stated).
  - Where: National branch network. Status: Running. Sources: [1](https://bracinternational.org/en/where-we-work/tanzania/)
- **ELA clubs (Empowerment and Livelihood for Adolescents)** (mentoring or incubation). Girls' clubs with life skills, livelihood training and (for some) a small loan.
  - Who: Girls aged 13-19. How: Through BRAC. Cost: Free.
  - Where: Was Iringa, Dodoma, Mbeya. Status: Pilot 2009-2013; current status not confirmed. Sources: [1](https://bigd.bracu.ac.bd/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/IERC-Research-Brief_Impact-of-the-Adolescent-Development-Programme-in-Tanzania.pdf)

## Moyo wa Mwanamke Shujaa (MwST)

- **Re-entry schooling with vocational and life skills** (school programme). A second chance at school, with vocational and life-skills training (481 girls back in school since 2015).
  - Who: Girls who left school. How: Contact the organisation. Cost: not stated.
  - Where: check. Status: Running (July 2026). Sources: [1](https://allafrica.com/stories/202607280238.html)

## Youth Alliance for Progress Organisation (YAPO)

- **Gairo alternative secondary education centre (with the Commonwealth of Learning)** (school programme). Secondary education for girls who left school because of pregnancy, with an inclusive learning environment.
  - Who: Young mothers and girls affected by early pregnancy. How: Through the Gairo centre. Cost: not stated.
  - Where: Gairo, Morogoro. Status: Check. Sources: [1](https://www.col.org/news/supporting-young-mothers-return-to-learning-enabling-tanzanian-teachers-to-build-inclusive-school-environment/)

## Doris Mollel Foundation

- **Neonatal equipment and training for hospitals** (organisation only). Incubators, oxygen machines, baby warmers and training for health workers (20+ hospitals supported).
  - Who: Hospitals caring for premature babies. How: Contact the foundation. Cost: Free to hospitals.
  - Where: National. Status: Running (2025). Sources: [1](https://ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/news/local-news/read/doris-mollel-championing-pushing-legal-reforms-for-preterm-baby-parents-in-tanzania-2025-02-03-210845)
- **Kwimba preterm care facility (mothers and babies together)** (clinic or health service). Care for premature babies with a ward where mothers stay with their babies.
  - Who: Mothers of premature babies. How: At Kwimba District Hospital. Cost: Public hospital fees (check).
  - Where: Kwimba, Mwanza. Status: Opened February 2026. Sources: [1](https://allafrica.com/stories/202602250039.html)
- **Mental health support for mothers of preterm babies (with Mental 360)** (clinic or health service). Mental health support for mothers of premature babies.
  - Who: Mothers of premature babies. How: Through the foundation (check). Cost: check.
  - Where: check. Status: Reported 2025. Sources: [1](https://ippmedia.co.tz/the-guardian/news/local-news/read/doris-mollels-mission-makes-history-as-who-marks-world-prematurity-day-2025-05-30-120320)
- **Return to school for young mothers (planned)** (school programme). Support for women who left school because of pregnancy to return to education.
  - Who: Adolescent girls and young mothers. How: Through councils (check). Cost: check.
  - Where: 47 councils in 15 regions (planned). Status: Announced (check start). Sources: [1](https://www.thecitizen.co.tz/tanzania/news/national/relief-for-adolescent-girls-and-young-mothers-who-dropped-out-of-school-5440296)
